About Us

Building strong
foundations since 2010.

Crystal Group is four companies working as one.C-ore Industries Limited quarries and crushes the stone. Crystal Premix Limited turns it into ready-mix concrete. Conecc Construction Limited moulds it into blocks, pavers and kerbstones.

Clover Construction Limited elevates it into premium, designer hardscape. Because every stage happens inside the group, quality is controlled from rock to ready and clients get one accountable partner instead of four separatesuppliers.
